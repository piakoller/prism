*   **Primary Tumor Resection:** The *ENETS 2024 guidelines* note that the role of prophylactic primary tumor resection in asymptomatic stage IV patients is controversial. While some retrospective data suggest a survival benefit, propensity-score matched studies show no benefit compared to no resection. *   **Combination Strategy:** The guidelines highlight recent retrospective studies indicating that the combination of Si-NET resection and PRRT provides the best disease-specific survival for patients with diffuse stage IV disease.
