**2. Completeness & Clinical Appropriateness:**
The patient has a Lung NET and has progressed on SSA and PRRT. The standard treatment landscape for Lung NETs includes Everolimus (RADIANT-4) and now Cabozantinib (CABINET).
- **Everolimus:** Established standard, generally better tolerated regarding GI toxicity (diarrhea), though carries risk of stomatitis/pneumonitis.
- **Cabozantinib:** Newer option, robust data in post-PRRT setting, but high toxicity (diarrhea, hypertension, PPE).
The recommendation exclusively selects Cabozantinib. Given the patient's specific report of "loose stools," initiating a therapy where diarrhea is the dose-limiting toxicity for the majority of patients is risky if a safer, approved alternative (Everolimus) exists and hasn't been used. The recommendation should present Everolimus as a viable option or explain why Cabozantinib is preferred despite the GI symptom risk (e.g., if the disease progression is considered rapid/bulky requiring higher response probability, though ORR for both is relatively low).

A complete recommendation would consider whether the presence of loose stools makes Everolimus a preferable starting point over Cabozantinib.
