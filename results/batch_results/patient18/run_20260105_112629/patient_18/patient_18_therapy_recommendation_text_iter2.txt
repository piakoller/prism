**Primary Systemic Therapy Recommendation:**
**Everolimus** (10 mg orally once daily).
*   **Evidence:** The **RADIANT-4 trial** (Phase 3) demonstrated a significant progression-free survival (PFS) benefit for Everolimus compared to placebo in non-functional lung and GI NETs (Median PFS **11.0 months** vs. 3.9 months; HR 0.48; P<0.001). The **LUNA trial (NCT01563354)** specifically evaluated Everolimus in lung and thymus NETs, confirming its activity (33.3% of patients progression-free at 9 months).
*   **Clinical Context:** Given the patient's current symptom of "loose stools," Everolimus is preferred as the initial choice over Cabozantinib, which has a significantly higher incidence of Grade 3-4 diarrhea (~62% in CABINET vs. lower rates with Everolimus).

**Strong Alternative Systemic Therapy:**
**Cabozantinib** (60 mg orally once daily).
*   **Evidence:** The Phase 3 **CABINET trial (NCT03375320)** provides high-level evidence for patients with advanced extrapancreatic NETs (including lung) who have progressed after prior therapy (including PRRT). In the extra-pancreatic NET cohort, Cabozantinib demonstrated a median PFS of **8.3 months** vs. 3.2 months with placebo (Hazard Ratio **0.45**; 95% CI, 0.30 to 0.66).
*   **Usage Note:** This is a potent option, particularly if the patient tolerates Everolimus poorly or progresses. However, the etiology of the patient's "loose stools" must be controlled first, as Cabozantinib is likely to exacerbate diarrhea.

**SSTR-Targeted Therapy Consideration (Retreatment or Alpha-Therapy):**
**PRRT Retreatment (177Lu-DOTATATE)** or **Targeted Alpha-Therapy (212Pb-DOTAMTATE)**.
*   **Evidence:** The patient achieved a **durable response** to prior PRRT (stability from ~2022 to March 2025, >2 years). Combined with the "intensive" tracer activity on the current DOTATOC PET/CT, the tumor biology remains highly SSTR-driven.
    *   **NCT02236910 (Registry):** Supports the utility of 177Lu-DOTATATE in patients with progressive NETs, particularly those with prior benefit.
    *   **NCT05153772 (AlphaMedix):** A Phase 2 trial of the alpha-emitter 212Pb-DOTAMTATE reported an Objective Response Rate (ORR) of ~55% in patients who had progressed on prior PRRT, offering a high-potency option for refractory disease.

**Symptom Management & Supportive Care:**
**Investigate and Treat "Loose Stools":**
*   **If Carcinoid Syndrome (CS) is confirmed:** Initiate **Telotristat ethyl** (250 mg three times daily) as an add-on to SSA. The real-world study **NCT03223428** and pivotal trials confirm its efficacy in reducing bowel movement frequency in refractory CS.
*   **If Non-CS (e.g., SSA-induced steatorrhea):** Optimize pancreatic enzyme replacement.
*   **Note:** Clarifying this is essential before initiating Cabozantinib to avoid compounding toxicity.

**Therapies to AVOID:**
*   **Single-agent Immunotherapy (PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors):** Do not use single-agent Nivolumab or Avelumab.
    *   **Evidence:** The **DART trial (NCT02834013)** showed a **0% ORR** in low/intermediate-grade NETs treated with Ipilimumab plus Nivolumab. **NCT03278379** showed **0% ORR** with Avelumab in G2-3 NETs.
*   **Ribociclib + Everolimus:** Do not use this combination.
    *   **Evidence:** **NCT03070301** demonstrated insufficient activity and significant toxicity in foregut NETs.
