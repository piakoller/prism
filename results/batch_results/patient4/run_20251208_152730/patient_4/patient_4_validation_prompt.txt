RECOMMENDATION:
**Primary Recommendation:**
Initiation of **Cabozantinib** (60 mg orally once daily) combined with continuation of **Somatostatin Analogue (SSA)** therapy.

**Evidence Support:**
This recommendation is supported by the Phase 3 **CABINET trial (NCT03375320)**, which demonstrated a significant improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) for patients with advanced extrapancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (epNETs) who had progressed on prior targeted therapy.
*   **Key Findings:** In the epNET cohort, Cabozantinib extended median PFS to **8.4 months** compared to 3.9 months with placebo (HR 0.38; 95% CI, 0.25 to 0.59; P<0.001).
