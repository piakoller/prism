  ✅ NCT03049189: Efficacy and Safety of 177Lu-edotreotide PRRT in GEP-NET Patients (COMPETE)
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial compares PRRT (177Lu-edotreotide) against Everolimus (a guideline-recommended option for this patient).      Rationale: This Phase 2 trial demonstrates clinical activity (ORR 15%, PFS ~14 months) of the combination of Atezolizumab and Bevacizumab in extrapancreatic NETs. This provides evidence for a combination strategy (VEGF + PD-L1 inhibition) as a potential alternative to standard single-agent therapies.
  ✅ NCT01842165: 177Lutetium-octreotate Treatment Prediction Using Multimodality Imaging in Refractory NETs (LUMEN)
     Rationale: This prospective Phase 2 trial provides high-quality data on the efficacy (PFS 28 months) and dosimetry of 177Lu-DOTATATE. This evidence supports the feasibility and potential benefit of PRRT, which is crucial for considering a re-challenge strategy.
  ✅ NCT03375320: Testing Cabozantinib in Patients With Advanced Pancreatic Neuroendocrine and Carcinoid Tumors (CABINET)
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial provides high-level evidence for Cabozantinib in patients with progressive extrapancreatic NETs who have received prior targeted therapy (like this patient). The significant PFS benefit (8.4 vs 3.9 months) makes this a primary candidate for the next line of therapy.
  ✅ NCT02795858: A Phase II Study of Ramucirumab With Somatostatin Analog Therapy in Patients With Advanced, Progressive Carcinoid Tumors
     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial demonstrates efficacy (PFS 14.2 months) of Ramucirumab (VEGFR2 antibody) in advanced NETs. It serves as supportive evidence for continued VEGF inhibition using a different mechanism (antibody vs. TKI) after progression on Sunitinib/Surufatinib.
