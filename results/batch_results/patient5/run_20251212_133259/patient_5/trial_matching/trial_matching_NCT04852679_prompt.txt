The patient presents with a colonic MiNEN (small-cell NEC component, Ki-67 90%) in clinical and pathological remission regarding the residual pararenal lesion, based on the recent negative biopsy showing only chronic inflammation. There is currently no histological evidence of active disease to justify immediate local treatment (surgery or radiotherapy).
