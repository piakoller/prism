**1. Current Management: Maintenance Therapy and Surveillance**
*   **Therapy:** Continue **Lanreotide Autogel 120 mg** deep subcutaneous injection every 28 days.
*   **Surveillance:**
    *   **Biochemical:** Monitor plasma or urinary 5-HIAA and **NT-proBNP** every 6 months. The diagnostic utility of plasma/urinary 5-HIAA is supported by **NCT02826928** and **ENETS 2022 Guidelines**.
    *   **Cardiac:** Perform transthoracic echocardiography every 6–12 months to screen for Carcinoid Heart Disease (CHD), given the patient's history of elevated 5-HIAA, as recommended by **ENETS 2022 Guidelines**.
    *   **Radiological:** CT or MRI of the abdomen/pelvis every 6 months (or 3-6 months per **ENETS 2024 Guidelines** for G2 tumors).

**2. Response to Tumor Board Question**
*   **Question:** If there is not a radiological PD, but the syndrome is not well controlled, is PRRT indicated?
*   **Recommendation:** **Yes, PRRT is indicated.**
*   **Evidence:** According to **ENETS 2022** and **ENETS 2024 Guidelines**, PRRT is a valid therapeutic option for Refractory Carcinoid Syndrome (RCS) even in the absence of radiological progressive disease (PD), provided there is sufficient uptake on SSTR imaging. PRRT has been documented to decrease diarrhea, flushing, and 5-HIAA levels.

**3. Future Therapeutic Options (Upon Progression or Recurrent Syndrome)**
*   **For Recurrent Syndrome (Diarrhea):**
    *   **Telotristat Ethyl:** If diarrhea recurs despite optimized SSA, add Telotristat Ethyl 250 mg t.i.d. (**ENETS 2024 Guidelines**).
*   **For Radiological Progression (Antiproliferative Therapy):**
    *   **Cabozantinib:** Supported by **NCT03375320 (CABINET)** and **ENETS 2024 Guidelines**, which note that Cabozantinib improved Progression-Free Survival (PFS) in patients with advanced extra-pancreatic NETs (HR 0.41).
    *   **Everolimus:** A standard option for progressive GI-NETs (**ESMO Guidelines**; **NCT03049189** context).
    *   **PRRT Rechallenge:** If the patient maintains SSTR expression and had a durable response (>12 months), retreatment may be considered (**ENETS 2024 Guidelines**). Future options may also include Alpha-emitter therapy (**NCT05153772**) or personalized dosimetry (**NCT02754297**).

**4. Periprocedural Safety**
*   **Recommendation:** For any future invasive procedures (biopsy, TAE) or PRRT cycles, administer **prophylactic short-acting octreotide** (s.c. 100–500 µg q 6–8 h or i.v. infusion) starting 12 hours pre-procedure and continuing until stabilization to prevent carcinoid crisis, given the patient's history of hypotensive episodes (**ENETS 2022 Guidelines**).
