**Primary Recommendation: Escalation to Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT) with 177Lu-DOTATATE combined with continued Somatostatin Analogue (SSA)**

Given the radiological progression of the SSTR-positive duodenal neuroendocrine tumor, the recommended course is to escalate systemic therapy to **177Lu-DOTATATE PRRT**. Standard clinical practice and guideline recommendations support **continuing the Somatostatin Analogue (SSA)** maintenance therapy during and after PRRT to maintain symptom control (if functional) and for potential synergistic anti-proliferative effects.

*   **Pre-treatment Verification:** Confirm that uptake in all new liver and lymph node metastases exceeds normal liver parenchyma uptake on the Gallium DOTATATE-PET/CT (liver SUVmax typically ~6–7) to ensure eligibility.
*   **Supporting Evidence:**
    *   **NCT02754297 (Phase 2, Personalized PRRT):** Demonstrated an **Objective Response Rate (ORR) of 23.1%** and stable disease in 33.3% of assessable patients with progressive NETs, supporting the efficacy of 177Lu-octreotate in this setting.
    *   **NCT02236910 (Registry Study):** Provided real-world evidence for 177Lu-DOTATATE in progressive well-differentiated NETs, reporting a median **Progression-Free Survival (PFS) of 34.1 months**. While retrospective, this supports the potential for durable disease control.
    *   **NCT03049189 (COMPETE Phase 3 Trial):** This ongoing trial directly compares PRRT against Everolimus in GEP-NETs, underscoring PRRT as a primary standard-of-care consideration for patients progressing on first-line therapy.

**Alternative Systemic Options (Targeted Therapy)**
If PRRT is not feasible (e.g., due to renal/bone marrow compromise) or is declined, the following targeted therapies are recommended:

1.  **Cabozantinib:**
    *   **Evidence:** **NCT03375320 (CABINET Phase 3 Trial):** In patients with advanced extra-pancreatic NETs (epNETs) progressing on prior therapy, Cabozantinib significantly improved median PFS to **8.4 months** compared to 3.9 months with placebo (HR 0.38; 95% CI, 0.25 to 0.59). This trial provides high-level evidence specifically relevant to the patient's duodenal (extra-pancreatic) primary.
2.  **Everolimus:**
    *   **Evidence:** Supported by the **RADIANT-4** trial (referenced in ESMO guidelines), Everolimus is recommended for progressive non-functional GI-NETs.

**Surgical Recommendation**
A third liver resection is **not recommended** at this time. The rapid recurrence (within 2 years of the 2023 resection) and the appearance of new extra-hepatic disease (lymph node metastases) indicate that the disease is systemically driven, making local control insufficient.
