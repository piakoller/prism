### ALL_ISSUES ISSUES:
  ❌ Semantic: **Unsafe Therapy Modification (SSA Discontinuation):** The recommendation explicitly states to "Switch from SSA maintenance to 177Lu-DOTATATE PRRT." This implies stopping the Somatostatin Analogue. Standard clinical practice (supported by ESMO/ENETS guidelines and the NETTER-1 trial paradigm) is to **continue** SSA therapy (often at standard doses) in combination with PRRT, both for symptom control (if functional) and potential synergistic tumor control. Discontinuing SSA in a metastatic patient could be detrimental.
  ❌ Semantic: **Methodologically Flawed Evidence Comparison:** The rationale argues for the superiority of PRRT by directly comparing the median PFS of **34.1 months** from a *retrospective single-center registry study* (NCT02236910) against the **8.4 months** from a *Phase 3 Randomized Controlled Trial* (NCT03375320). This is a statistical fallacy. Retrospective data is subject to selection bias and cannot be directly compared to RCT data to determine superiority. While PRRT is an appropriate choice, this specific evidence comparison is misleading and overstates the certainty of the benefit magnitude relative to other options.
