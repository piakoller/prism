     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial provides critical negative evidence, showing that single-agent PD-L1 blockade (Avelumab) has limited antitumor activity in G2-3 NETs. This informs the recommendation by supporting the exclusion of immunotherapy as a viable treatment option for this patient at this time.
  ✅ NCT03049189: Efficacy and Safety of 177Lu-edotreotide PRRT in GEP-NET Patients
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial demonstrates superior progression-free survival of PRRT (177Lu-edotreotide) compared to everolimus. It supports the recommendation of PRRT as a preferred therapeutic class over switching to an mTOR inhibitor for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03217097: MGMT-NET: O6-methylguanine-DNA Methyltransferase (MGMT) Status in Neuroendocrine Tumors: Predictive Factor of Response to Alkylating Agents
     Rationale: This randomized Phase 2 trial provides evidence supporting the activity of alkylating chemotherapy (like temozolomide) in NETs and highlights the potential utility of MGMT status in selecting between alkylating agents and oxaliplatin, informing the chemotherapy option.
  ✅ NCT03375320: Testing Cabozantinib in Patients With Advanced Pancreatic Neuroendocrine and Carcinoid Tumors
     Rationale: This pivotal Phase 3 trial (CABINET) demonstrates a significant progression-free survival benefit of Cabozantinib in patients with progressive pNETs (including those with prior targeted therapy), establishing it as a strong evidence-based option for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03972488: Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Lutathera in Patients With Grade 2 and Grade 3 Advanced GEP-NET
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial (NETTER-2) establishes the efficacy of 177Lu-Dotatate specifically in G2 (Ki-67 10-55%) NETs. It directly addresses the patient's specific tumor grade (G2) and proliferation index (Ki-67 15%), strongly supporting PRRT eligibility.

     Rationale: This trial evaluates an investigational alpha-emitter (212Pb-DOTAMTATE). Since the patient is PRRT-naive and eligible for standard beta-emitting PRRT (Lutathera), which has Phase 3 evidence, this investigational therapy is not the primary recommendation.

     Rationale: This is a retrospective registry study. Evidence for the efficacy of Lu-DOTA-TATE is better derived from the available randomized Phase 3 trials (NETTER-2) rather than registry data.

     Rationale: This approach requires hormone receptor expression (not documented for this patient) and demonstrated only modest efficacy. It is not a standard-of-care option compared to PRRT or targeted therapies.
