Based on the patient's clinical status (progressive G2 Pan-NET, Ki-67 15%) and the analyzed trial evidence, the following management strategy is recommended:

**1. Diagnostic Work-up (Urgent)**
*   **Action:** Perform a **Somatostatin Receptor (SSTR) PET/CT** (e.g., 68Ga-DOTATATE or 68Ga-DOTATOC) immediately.
*   **Purpose:** To evaluate the reported "migratory bone pain" for bone metastases (which are poorly detected by CT) and to confirm SSTR expression levels for PRRT eligibility.

**2. Liver Transplantation (LT) Feasibility**
*   **Recommendation:** **Liver transplantation is NOT recommended** for this patient.
*   **Reasoning:** The patient does not meet the strict ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines criteria for LT:
    *   **Tumor Biology:** Her Ki-67 index is **15%**, which exceeds the absolute threshold of **<10%** required for LT eligibility.
    *   **Disease Stability:** She has active hepatic progression; guidelines require stable disease for at least 6 months prior to transplant consideration.
    *   **Extrahepatic Risk:** The uninvestigated bone pain presents a high risk of extrahepatic disease, which is an absolute contraindication for LT.

**3. Systemic Therapy Recommendation (Primary)**
*   **Recommendation:** Initiate **Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT) with 177Lu-Dotatate**.
*   **Condition:** This therapy is indicated upon confirmation of SSTR-positive disease, defined as uptake in tumor lesions significantly higher than the liver background (Krenning score 3 or 4).
*   **Evidence (NETTER-2, NCT03972488):** This Phase 3 trial specifically evaluated 177Lu-Dotatate in patients with G2 (Ki-67 10–55%) and G3 GEP-NETs. The trial demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in **median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) to 22.8 months** compared to 8.5 months with high-dose octreotide (HR 0.276; p<0.0001).
*   **Relevance:** This patient’s specific tumor proliferation index (Ki-67 15%) places her directly within the population that derived substantial benefit in this trial. PRRT is the recommended systemic treatment regardless of whether the disease is liver-limited or involves the bones, provided SSTR expression is sufficient.

**4. Alternative Therapy (if SSTR-negative)**
*   **Recommendation:** Initiate **Cabozantinib** (60 mg daily).
*   **Evidence (CABINET, NCT03375320):** This Phase 3 trial demonstrated that Cabozantinib significantly improved **median PFS to 13.8 months** versus 4.4 months with placebo (HR 0.23) in patients with progressive pancreatic NETs, including those who had received prior targeted therapy. This serves as a strong evidence-based alternative if PRRT is not feasible.

**5. Therapies to Exclude**
*   **Immunotherapy (PD-L1 blockade):** Based on trial **NCT03278379** (Avelumab), single-agent PD-L1 blockade showed limited antitumor activity (ORR 0%) in G2-3 NETs and is not recommended.
