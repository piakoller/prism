     Rationale: The PRODIGE 41-BEVANEC trial specifically addresses second-line treatment for GEP-NEC after platinum-etoposide failure.   ✅ NCT02834013: Nivolumab and Ipilimumab in Treating Patients With Rare Tumors
     Rationale: The DART trial's high-grade neuroendocrine neoplasm cohort showed a 26-44% objective response rate with dual checkpoint inhibition (Nivolumab + Ipilimumab). This provides strong evidence for using dual immunotherapy as a therapeutic strategy for high-grade NEC, particularly if chemotherapy options are exhausted or declined.
  ✅ NCT03837977: Second-line Therapy for Patients With Progressive Poorly Differentiated Extra-pulmonary Neuroendocrine Carcinoma
     Rationale: The NET-02 trial demonstrated that Liposomal Irinotecan (nal-IRI) plus 5-FU/Folinic Acid met the primary endpoint for progression-free survival in progressive PD-EP-NEC, whereas Docetaxel did not. This identifies a specific, evidence-based second-line chemotherapy regimen for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03136055: Pembrolizumab-based Therapy in Previously Treated High Grade Neuroendocrine Carcinomas
     Rationale: This study showed that Pembrolizumab, either alone or with chemotherapy, had minimal efficacy (ORR 5-7%) in extrapulmonary high-grade NEC. This is crucial negative evidence that helps rule out Pembrolizumab-based regimens in favor of more effective combinations (like Ipi/Nivo or Nivo/TMZ) for this patient.
