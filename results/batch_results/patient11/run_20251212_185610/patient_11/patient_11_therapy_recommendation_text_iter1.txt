**Immediate Action: Molecular Profiling**
Before initiating further systemic therapy or consolidation, the following biomarker testing must be performed on the available tumor tissue (liver biopsy or primary resection specimen):
*   **Microsatellite Instability (MSI) / Mismatch Repair (MMR) status**
*   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB)**
*   **Rationale:** According to ENETS 2023 guidelines (Q2, Q8), these are critical predictive biomarkers for immunotherapy. While rare in pancreatic NEC (~5%), the presence of MSI-High or TMB-High status would strongly support the use of immune checkpoint inhibitors in later lines of therapy.

**Primary Recommendation: Local Consolidation of Oligometastatic Disease**
Given the patient's **Partial Response (PR)** to first-line platinum-etoposide and current **oligometastatic status** (single 20 × 12 mm hepatic lesion in segment VII), the recommended strategy is local consolidation to achieve a disease-free state.
*   **Preferred Modality:** **Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)** or **Stereotactic Body Radiation Therapy (SBRT)**.
    *   **Reasoning:** While surgical resection is an option for highly selected responders (ENETS 2023 Q7), this patient's history of **valvular heart disease** (aortic, mitral, and tricuspid regurgitation) and ventricular extrasystoles presents elevated perioperative risk. A minimally invasive ablative approach offers local control with a safer profile for a single 2 cm lesion.
*   **Surgical Consideration:** If ablation is technically not feasible, surgical metastasectomy may be considered only after rigorous **cardiology clearance**.

**Secondary Recommendation: Second-Line Systemic Therapy (Upon Progression)**
When the patient experiences disease progression, the recommended evidence-based regimen is **Liposomal Irinotecan (nal-IRI) plus 5-Fluorouracil (5-FU) and Folinic Acid**.
*   **Evidence:** The **NET-02 Phase II trial (NCT03837977)** demonstrated that nal-IRI/5-FU/folinic acid met the primary endpoint for 6-month progression-free survival (PFS) rate (29.6% vs. 13.8% with Docetaxel) in patients with progressive poorly differentiated extra-pulmonary neuroendocrine carcinoma.
*   **Alternative:** Standard **FOLFIRI** (Irinotecan + 5-FU) is also a validated option supported by the control arm of the **PRODIGE 41-BEVANEC trial (NCT02820857)**, which showed no survival benefit to adding Bevacizumab.

**Alternative Systemic Options (Immunotherapy)**
*   **Dual Immunotherapy (Nivolumab + Ipilimumab):** Recommended specifically if the patient is found to be **MSI-High/TMB-High**, or within a clinical trial setting for unselected patients.
    *   **Evidence:** The **DART trial (SWOG S1609, NCT02834013)** reported an Objective Response Rate (ORR) of **26%** in the dedicated high-grade neuroendocrine neoplasm cohort (PMID: 33882143) and **44%** in the non-pancreatic high-grade cohort (PMID: 31969335). This suggests potential for durable response in refractory high-grade disease, though efficacy in microsatellite-stable pancreatic NEC remains variable.
*   **Nivolumab + Temozolomide:** This combination showed an ORR of **32%** in neuroendocrine neoplasms in trial **NCT03728361** and serves as another viable option for immune-sensitized tumors.
*   **Contraindication:** Single-agent PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors (e.g., Pembrolizumab, Avelumab) are **not recommended** for microsatellite-stable disease due to lack of efficacy (ORR <10%) demonstrated in trials **NCT03278379** and **NCT03136055**.
