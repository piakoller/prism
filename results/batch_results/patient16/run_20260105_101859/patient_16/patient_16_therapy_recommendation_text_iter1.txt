Based on the patient's diagnosis of a locally advanced, Grade 1 (Ki-67 2%), SSTR2-positive nonfunctioning pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor (NF-Pan-NET) with oligometastatic liver and lymph node involvement, the recommended therapy is a **neoadjuvant approach using Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT)** with **177Lu-DOTATATE/Edotreotide**, followed by re-evaluation for **curative-intent surgical resection**.

Concurrently, the patient should initiate **Somatostatin Analogue (SSA)** therapy (e.g., **Lanreotide** or **Octreotide**) for immediate antiproliferative control and symptom management.

**Key Clinical Trial Evidence Supporting This Recommendation:**
*   **NCT02754297 (Personalized PRRT of Neuroendocrine Tumors):** This Phase 2 trial provides the primary evidence for the neoadjuvant strategy in this specific patient population. The study demonstrated that personalized PRRT dosimetry resulted in high response rates, specifically reporting that **84.6% (11 of 13) of patients with pancreatic NETs achieved a partial or minor response**. This high rate of tumor shrinkage is critical for this patient to facilitate downstaging of the pancreatic head lesion (currently contacting the portal vein) and to address the oligometastatic disease prior to surgery.
*   **NCT01842165 (Prediction Using Multimodality Imaging):** This completed study validates that high uptake on SSTR-PET/CT—which this patient exhibits with "intense accumulation"—is a reliable predictor of favorable treatment outcome with 177Lu-octreotate, confirming her suitability for this modality.
*   **NCT03049189 (COMPETE Trial):** This Phase 3 trial supports the therapeutic positioning of **177Lu-edotreotide PRRT** as a superior consideration over targeted agents (such as everolimus) for SSTR-positive GEP-NETs, reinforcing the selection of PRRT in the treatment sequence.
*   **NCT02730104 (Lanreotide Real-World Study):** This observational study confirms the real-world effectiveness and safety of Lanreotide in GEP-NETs, supporting its use as a standard first-line option for antiproliferative control and symptom management alongside PRRT.

**Surgical Re-evaluation Plan:**
Following the induction course of PRRT (typically evaluated after 2-4 cycles), the patient should undergo full restaging. If sufficient downstaging is achieved to allow for an R0 resection (clear margins) of the primary tumor and the retroperitoneal lymph nodes, a **pancreaticoduodenectomy** combined with **resection or ablation of the liver metastases** is recommended.
