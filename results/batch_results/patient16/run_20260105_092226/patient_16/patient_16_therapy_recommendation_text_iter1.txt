Based on the patient's clinical profile (symptomatic, G1, SSTR-positive NF-Pan-NET with oligometastatic liver and nodal disease) and the provided evidence, the recommended therapy is:

**1. Primary Strategy: Surgical Resection (If R0 Feasible)**
**Recommendation:** Proceed with **pancreaticoduodenectomy (Whipple procedure)** combined with **resection or ablation of liver metastases** and regional lymphadenectomy.
*   **Condition:** This is indicated only if the multidisciplinary surgical team confirms that a curative (R0) resection is technically feasible, specifically addressing the 3 cm contact with the portal/superior mesenteric vein and the retroperitoneal paraaortic lymph nodes.
*   **Guideline Support:** ENETS guidelines strongly recommend surgery for NF-Pan-NETs >2 cm or those associated with main pancreatic duct dilation (both present here). The guidelines also state that radical resection including venous resection can be performed safely in expert centers.

**2. Neoadjuvant Strategy: Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT) (If R0 Not Feasible)**
**Recommendation:** If R0 resection is assessed as not immediately feasible due to the vascular contact or retroperitoneal nodal extent, administer **neoadjuvant PRRT with 177Lu-DOTATATE**.
*   **Trial Evidence:**
    *   **NCT02754297 (Personalized PRRT):** According to the publication abstract included in the trial evidence (Del Prete et al.), this Phase 2 trial demonstrated that personalized PRRT achieved a **partial or minor response in 84.6% (11 of 13)** of patients with pancreatic NETs. This high response rate supports the utility of PRRT for tumor shrinkage (downstaging) to facilitate future resection.
    *   **NCT02236910:** This registry study reported a median progression-free survival (PFS) of **34.1 months** in well-differentiated NETs treated with 177Lu-DOTATATE, supporting its efficacy in this patient population.
*   **Guideline Support:** ENETS guidelines explicitly suggest that preoperative treatment with PRRT may be considered in selected cases of locally advanced or oligometastatic SSTR-positive NF-Pan-NETs to facilitate R0 resection.
*   **Eligibility:** The patient's DOTATOC PET/CT shows "intense accumulation," satisfying the requirement for high SSTR expression.

**3. Systemic Therapy: Somatostatin Analogues (SSAs)**
**Recommendation:** Initiate **Lanreotide Autogel (120 mg)** or **Octreotide LAR (30 mg)** every 4 weeks for antiproliferative control and symptom management.
*   **Trial Evidence:**
    *   **NCT02730104:** The prospective observational study by Paulson et al. demonstrated a **24-month PFS rate of 73.7%** in patients with GEP-NETs treated with Lanreotide, confirming its real-world effectiveness and safety profile.
*   **Guideline Support:** ESMO and ENETS guidelines recommend SSAs as first-line antiproliferative therapy for SSTR-positive, well-differentiated (G1) NF-Pan-NETs.
