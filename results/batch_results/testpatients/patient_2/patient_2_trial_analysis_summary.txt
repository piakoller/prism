TRIALS TO INCLUDE IN RECOMMENDATION:
  ✅ NCT03049189: Efficacy and Safety of 177Lu-edotreotide PRRT in GEP-NET Patients
     Rationale: The COMPETE trial is a pivotal Phase 3 study showing that PRRT provides a statistically significant progression-free survival (PFS) benefit over everolimus, a standard first-line option. This high-level evidence directly compares two relevant therapies for this patient.
  ✅ NCT05153772: Targeted Alpha-emitter Therapy of PRRT Naïve and Previous PRRT Neuroendocrine Tumor Patients
     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial of a novel alpha-emitter PRRT (212Pb-DOTAMTATE) showed a high objective response rate (56.8%) in PRRT-naive patients. This is a highly promising result for a symptomatic patient with a high tumor burden who would benefit from tumor shrinkage.
  ✅ NCT03074513: Atezolizumab and Bevacizumab in Treating Patients With Rare Solid Tumors
     Rationale: This trial provides evidence for a non-chemotherapy, non-PRRT option, showing that the combination of atezolizumab and bevacizumab achieved a 20% objective response rate and a median PFS of 14.9 months in patients with pancreatic NETs.
  ✅ NCT02754297: Personalized PRRT of Neuroendocrine Tumors
     Rationale: This study supports the efficacy of PRRT, particularly in pancreatic NETs, and introduces the concept of personalized dosimetry to potentially maximize tumor irradiation safely, which is a valuable consideration for optimizing treatment.
  ✅ NCT03728361: Nivolumab and Temozolomide in Treating Patients With Recurrent or Refractory Small-Cell Lung Cancer or Advanced Neuroendocrine Cancer
     Rationale: This trial demonstrates promising activity for a chemo-immunotherapy combination (nivolumab + temozolomide). As temozolomide is a component of a standard-of-care regimen for this patient, this combination is a relevant therapeutic option to consider.
  ✅ NCT03217097: MGMT-NET: O6-methylguanine-DNA Methyltransferase (MGMT) Status in Neuroendocrine Tumors: Predictive Factor of Response to Alkylating Agents
     Rationale: This randomized trial provides critical evidence that the efficacy of alkylating agent-based chemotherapy (e.g., temozolomide) is significantly higher in patients with dMGMT (deficient/methylated) tumors, making MGMT status a key predictive biomarker for treatment selection.
  ✅ NCT01842165: 177Lutetium-octreotate Treatment Prediction Using Multimodality Imaging in Refractory NETs
     Rationale: The LUMEN study confirms the significant efficacy of PRRT in GEP-NETs (median PFS of 28 months) and identifies dosimetric and early imaging biomarkers for response, reinforcing PRRT as a strong, evidence-based option for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03972488: Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Lutathera in Patients With Grade 2 and Grade 3 Advanced GEP-NET
     Rationale: This is a landmark, practice-changing Phase 3 trial (NETTER-2) that provides Level 1 evidence for using PRRT as a first-line therapy in this exact patient population (newly diagnosed, advanced G2 NET with Ki-67 ≥10%), showing a profound PFS benefit over high-dose SSAs.

TRIALS TO EXCLUDE FROM RECOMMENDATION:
  ❌ NCT03278379: Avelumab in G2-3 NET
     Rationale: The published results for this trial of single-agent PD-L1 blockade showed "limited antitumour activity" with no objective responses observed, making it an unsuitable option for a patient requiring an effective first-line therapy.
  ❌ NCT02730104: Community-based Neuroendocrine Tumor (NET) Research Study
     Rationale: This observational study on lanreotide (an SSA) does not support its use as a first-line monotherapy for this patient, as guidelines recommend more active treatment for high-burden, symptomatic G2 NETs with a Ki-67 >10%.
  ❌ NCT02568267: Basket Study of Entrectinib (RXDX-101) for the Treatment of Patients With Solid Tumors Harboring NTRK 1/2/3 (Trk A/B/C), ROS1, or ALK Gene Rearrangements (Fusions)
     Rationale: This trial is only relevant for patients with NTRK, ROS1, or ALK gene fusions. As these specific molecular alterations have not been identified in the patient, this targeted therapy is not applicable.
  ❌ NCT02236910: An Open Label Registry Study of Lutetium-177 (DOTA0, TYR3) Octreotate (Lu-DOTA-TATE) Treatment in Patients with Somatostatin Receptor Positive Tumours
     Rationale: This is a retrospective review whose findings on PRRT are superseded by more robust, prospective, and practice-changing randomized controlled trials that have been included in this analysis (e.g., NETTER-2, COMPETE).
  ❌ NCT03870399: Study of Tamoxifen in Well Differentiated Neuroendocrine Tumors and Hormone Receptor Positive Expression
     Rationale: The HORMONET trial of tamoxifen demonstrated only "modest antitumor effects" with no objective responses, making it an inappropriate choice for a patient with symptomatic, high-burden disease requiring a more active therapy.

     Rationale: The DART trial showed a 0% objective response rate for the combination of ipilimumab and nivolumab in patients with low/intermediate-grade non-pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors, suggesting a lack of efficacy for this patient's G2 tumor.
  ❌ NCT03375320: Testing Cabozantinib in Patients With Advanced Pancreatic Neuroendocrine and Carcinoid Tumors
     Rationale: The CABINET trial evaluated cabozantinib in previously treated patients, not in the first-line setting. While the data are promising for subsequent lines of therapy, they do not directly inform the immediate first-line treatment decision.
  ❌ NCT03070301: A Study of LEE011 With Everolimus in Patients With Advanced Neuroendocrine Tumors
     Rationale: This trial combining ribociclib with everolimus failed to meet its primary endpoint and showed insufficient activity in foregut NETs, demonstrating that this combination is not a viable therapeutic option.
  ❌ NCT01253161: Study of Pasireotide Long Acting Release (LAR) in Patients With Metastatic Neuroendocrine Tumors (NETs)
     Rationale: This trial of pasireotide showed modest efficacy (PFS 11 months) and a high rate of hyperglycemia. More effective and better-tolerated first-line options are available for this high-risk patient.

     Rationale: The LUNA trial is not relevant to this patient's case as it exclusively enrolled patients with neuroendocrine tumors of the lung and thymus, whereas this patient has a pancreatic primary.
  ❌ NCT02893930: Sapanisertib in Treating Patients With Metastatic or Refractory Pancreatic Neuroendocrine Tumor That Cannot Be Removed by Surgery
     Rationale: This trial evaluated sapanisertib in patients who were already resistant to mTOR inhibitors like everolimus and was terminated for lack of efficacy, making it irrelevant for a first-line treatment decision.
