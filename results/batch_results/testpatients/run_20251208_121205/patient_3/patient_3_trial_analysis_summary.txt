TRIALS TO INCLUDE IN RECOMMENDATION:
  ✅ NCT03049189: Efficacy and Safety of 177Lu-edotreotide PRRT in GEP-NET Patients (COMPETE)
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial provides high-quality evidence comparing PRRT (177Lu-edotreotide) directly against Everolimus, a standard targeted therapy. It establishes the efficacy superiority (PFS) of PRRT in GEP-NETs, which is crucial for weighing the benefits of attempting PRRT despite the patient's renal risks versus switching to a targeted agent.
  ✅ NCT05153772: Targeted Alpha-emitter Therapy of PRRT Naïve and Previous PRRT Neuroendocrine Tumor Patients (ALPHAMEDIX02)
     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial evaluates Alpha-emitter PRRT (212Pb-DOTAMTATE), reporting a favorable safety profile with low incidence of renal toxicity. Given the patient's significant renal impairment (Creatinine 2.24 mg/dL), evidence of a potentially less nephrotoxic PRRT alternative is highly relevant for treatment planning.
  ✅ NCT02236910: An Open Label Registry Study of Lutetium-177 (DOTA0, TYR3) Octreotate (Lu-DOTA-TATE) Treatment in Patients with Somatostatin Receptor Positive Tumours
     Rationale: This registry study identifies high liver tumor burden (>50% volume) as a predictor of poor overall survival with PRRT. Since the patient has extensive liver metastases (>25 lesions), this real-world evidence is valuable for prognosticating outcomes and managing expectations regarding PRRT efficacy.
  ✅ NCT03074513: Atezolizumab and Bevacizumab in Treating Patients With Rare Solid Tumors
     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial demonstrates clinical activity of the Atezolizumab/Bevacizumab combination specifically in pancreatic NETs. It provides evidence for an immunotherapy-based alternative, which is valuable if standard targeted therapies or PRRT are contraindicated due to the patient's comorbidities.
  ✅ NCT02754297: Personalized PRRT of Neuroendocrine Tumors (P-PRRT)
     Rationale: This trial investigates personalized dosimetry for PRRT to optimize tumor dose while limiting renal toxicity. This is critically important for this patient, whose renal impairment (Cr 2.24 mg/dL) might otherwise contraindicate standard fixed-dose PRRT; this evidence supports the feasibility of a dosimetry-guided approach.
  ✅ NCT03728361: Nivolumab and Temozolomide in Treating Patients With Recurrent or Refractory Small-Cell Lung Cancer or Advanced Neuroendocrine Cancer
     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial evaluates Nivolumab plus Temozolomide. Since Temozolomide is hepatically metabolized and generally safer for kidneys than other chemotherapies (like Capecitabine or Cisplatin), this combination represents a renal-sparing systemic option for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03217097: MGMT-NET: O6-methylguanine-DNA Methyltransferase (MGMT) Status in Neuroendocrine Tumors: Predictive Factor of Response to Alkylating Agents
     Rationale: This trial compares alkylating agents (Temozolomide) vs. Oxaliplatin based on MGMT status. It provides evidence for selecting the optimal chemotherapy regimen; notably, Oxaliplatin carries renal risks, so data supporting Temozolomide (an alkylating agent) efficacy is particularly relevant for this patient's safety profile.
  ✅ NCT01842165: 177Lutetium-octreotate Treatment Prediction Using Multimodality Imaging in Refractory NETs (LUMEN)
     Rationale: Similar to the P-PRRT trial, this study focuses on dosimetry and safety, confirming a lack of severe nephrotoxicity in its cohort. It reinforces the evidence base for using dosimetry to safely administer PRRT, addressing the primary barrier to treatment for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03375320: Testing Cabozantinib in Patients With Advanced Pancreatic Neuroendocrine and Carcinoid Tumors (CABINET)
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial provides high-level evidence that Cabozantinib significantly improves progression-free survival in progressive pancreatic NETs. As a potent TKI with a different profile than Everolimus, it represents a strong, evidence-based second-line or subsequent treatment option for this patient.
