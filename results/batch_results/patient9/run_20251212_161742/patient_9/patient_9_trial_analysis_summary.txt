TRIALS TO INCLUDE IN RECOMMENDATION:
  ✅ NCT05153772: Targeted Alpha-emitter Therapy of PRRT Naïve and Previous PRRT Neuroendocrine Tumor Patients
     Rationale: This trial provides clinical evidence for 212Pb-DOTAMTATE (Alpha-PRRT) in patients who have progressed on prior PRRT. The results indicate a favorable safety profile with low renal toxicity, making this a highly relevant evidence source for a fragile patient with renal impairment (EGFR 38 ml/min) who needs a new therapeutic line.
  ✅ NCT03074513: Atezolizumab and Bevacizumab in Treating Patients With Rare Solid Tumors
     Rationale: This Phase 2 trial provides evidence for the efficacy of the Atezolizumab/Bevacizumab combination in advanced pNETs (ORR 20%, PFS ~15 months). While Bevacizumab carries renal risks that require caution, this regimen represents a potential non-standard option for refractory disease that should be evaluated against the patient's renal status.
  ✅ NCT02754297: Personalized PRRT of Neuroendocrine Tumors
     Rationale: This study demonstrates that personalizing PRRT activity based on dosimetry is safe and effective. This evidence is critical for supporting a PRRT retreatment strategy, as the patient's significant renal impairment (EGFR 38 ml/min) necessitates a dosimetry-guided approach to avoid toxicity rather than standard fixed dosing.
  ✅ NCT01842165: 177Lutetium-octreotate Treatment Prediction Using Multimodality Imaging in Refractory NETs
     Rationale: This prospective study confirms the safety profile of 177Lu-DOTATATE and investigates dosimetric predictors of outcome. It provides supportive evidence that PRRT can be administered safely even in complex cases, reinforcing the feasibility of dosimetry-guided PRRT strategies for this patient.
  ✅ NCT03375320: Testing Cabozantinib in Patients With Advanced Pancreatic Neuroendocrine and Carcinoid Tumors
     Rationale: This Phase 3 trial (CABINET) provides high-level evidence that Cabozantinib significantly improves progression-free survival in patients with progressive pNETs who have received prior therapy (including PRRT). This establishes Cabozantinib as a strong, evidence-based standard of care option for this patient's next line of therapy.
