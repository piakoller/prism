**Primary Recommendation: Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT) Retreatment**
*   **Regimen:** 177Lu-DOTATATE (Retreatment cycles, typically 2-4 cycles).
*   **Evidence Basis:** **ENETS 2023 Guidance Paper (Section Q5)** and **ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines**.
*   **Prerequisites for Eligibility:**
    1.  **Somatostatin Receptor Imaging (SRI):** A new **68Ga-DOTATATE/TOC PET/CT** must confirm high tracer uptake (SUVmax > liver background) in all metastatic liver lesions to ensure the target is still present and dominant.
    2.  **Bone Marrow Reserve:** A thorough hematological assessment is mandatory. Although the patient currently has Grade 1 thrombocytopenia (recovering from Grade 2), guidelines warn of cumulative bone marrow toxicity (myelodysplasia/leukemia risk ~3-4%). Retreatment is contingent upon adequate reserve to withstand potential myelosuppression.
    3.  **Renal Function:** Verification of adequate renal function is required per standard PRRT protocols.

**Alternative Recommendation: Cabozantinib (Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitor)**
*   **Status:** **High-Risk Alternative** (To be considered only if PRRT retreatment is not feasible due to low SRI uptake or bone marrow contraindications).
*   **Evidence Basis:** **NCT03375320 (CABINET Phase 3 Trial)**.
*   **Dosing Strategy:** Initiate at a **reduced starting dose** (e.g., 40 mg or 20 mg daily) rather than the standard 60 mg.
    *   *Justification:* The patient has demonstrated intolerance to the TKI class (Sunitinib) with significant asthenia and thrombocytopenia. A reduced dose is critical to mitigate the risk of recurrent Grade 3-4 toxicity while attempting to leverage the efficacy seen in the CABINET trial.
*   **Efficacy Data:** In the Phase 3 CABINET trial (NCT03375320), patients with advanced pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (pNETs) who had progressed after prior therapy achieved a **median progression-free survival (PFS) of 13.8 months** with Cabozantinib compared to 4.4 months with placebo (Stratified HR 0.23; 95% CI, 0.12 to 0.42; P<0.001).
